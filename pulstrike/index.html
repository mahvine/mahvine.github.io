PULSTRIKE

A finger-based rhythm game for your Meta VR devices, Turn any surface into a grooving playground

PULSTRIKE is a rhythm game for Meta Quest played with your hands, no controllers. Sit at a desk or table, rest your palms on it for a moment, and the game fits its play area to the surface. Notes appear on it with a ring closing in on each one: tap the note as the ring lands, on the beat. Every tap lands on something solid. No table? Play in the air instead, and tilt the play area towards you.

Features

  • Hands only. Tap with your index finger, middle finger or thumb. Yellow notes are for your left hand, teal for your right, white for either.
  • Sliders and drums. Follow orbs across the table with a fingertip, and pound the desk with your fist on the drum notes.
  • Original music. Eight songs made for the game, each with Easy, Normal and Hard charts, unlocked as you clear them.
  • Overload. Build your combo and your hands charge up; at 100, 200 and 300 the table erupts and every hit scores more.
  • Learn in a minute. A short tutorial teaches each kind of note, freezing time to show you the moment to tap.
  • Mixed reality. Play with your real room around you, or switch to a calm dark space.
  • Made for sitting down. Everything is within reach of your chair; a song takes two to three minutes.

What you need

A Meta Quest 2, Quest 3 or Quest 3S with hand tracking on, and a clear desk or table about 60 × 35 cm, or some room in front of you to play in the air.
