PULSTRIKE

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

PULSTRIKE ("the game") is a rhythm game for Meta Quest made by BanabaStudios ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the game uses, what it keeps, and what it does not do.

In short: the game does not collect personal information and sends nothing to us. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. What it keeps stays on your headset.

What the game uses while you play

  • Hand tracking: where your hands and fingertips are, so you can tap notes and press the game's buttons.
  • Head position: where your head is, to place the play area in front of you and keep it in view.
  • Your desk or play area: its position, measured from your hands when you set it up. The game asks your headset to keep a spatial anchor (a marker in the headset's map of your room) so the play area stays where you put it between sessions.
  • Your room setup (only if you allow the game to use spatial data when your headset asks): the desks and tables from your headset's room setup, as rectangles with a position and size, to line the play area up with your desk. The game does not keep this.
  • Passthrough: when mixed reality is on, the headset shows your room behind the game. The game does not receive, record or keep camera images.
  • Ownership check: when the game starts, it asks Meta's platform services on your headset whether your Meta account owns the game. The game learns only whether it does.

What the game keeps on your headset

  • Your settings and progress: timing offset, volumes, mixed reality on or off, whether you play on a surface or in the air and the table's tilt, best scores, unlocked songs, whether you have seen the tutorial, and the identifier of the play area's spatial anchor.
  • Session logs: text files in the game's own folder on the headset, recording how each session went so the game can be tuned: notes hit and missed and by how much, fingertip and head positions during play, where the play area was placed, and the frame rate. They contain no name, account, voice, camera images or contact details.

None of this leaves your headset unless you copy it off yourself, for example over USB. Uninstalling the game deletes it. Your headset keeps spatial anchors in its own room data, which you can clear in the headset's settings.

What the game does not do

  • It does not send any data to us or to any third party.
  • It does not collect your name, email, account details, location, contacts, voice or images.
  • It has no advertising or analytics, and does not sell or share data.

Meta

Your headset and Meta's services, including hand tracking, passthrough, room setup and the store, handle data under Meta's own privacy policy, not this one: meta.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Playtests

If you take part in a playtest, we may ask you to share the session logs from the headset you used, and a feedback form you choose to fill in. We use them only to improve the game, keep them no longer than 12 months, and delete them sooner if you ask.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.
